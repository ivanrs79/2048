Privacy Policy

2048 Stone Clash Multiplayer ("the game"), available at ivanrs79.github.io/2048 and as an Android app.
Last updated: 29 September 2026.

Summary

The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, your current solo game and your last selected mode are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data.

Online play

Online mode connects you directly to the other player using WebRTC, through the open-source PeerJS library. To make that connection:

  • Your device connects to the PeerJS matchmaking server (0.peerjs.com) using a random, temporary room code. That server sees your IP address while you are connected.
  • Your device may contact a public STUN server run by Google (stun.l.google.com) to find a network route, and, if a direct connection is not possible, PeerJS relay servers (turn.peerjs.com) that pass the game data along.
  • During a match, your board, score and moves are sent to your opponent. As with any peer-to-peer connection, your opponent's device can see your IP address.

No names, contacts or other personal information are sent. These third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
